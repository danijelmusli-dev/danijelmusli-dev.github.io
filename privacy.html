Privacy Policy

BoilerClips Uploader · Last updated: 29 September 2026

What this app is

BoilerClips Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the BoilerClips YouTube channel (@boiler_clips) to upload and schedule that channel's own videos. It is not offered to other users.

Google user data the app accesses

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos and set their title, description, tags, privacy and publish time on the owner's channel.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the connected channel's name and ID, and the status of videos the app uploaded.

How the data is used and stored

  • The OAuth token and channel name are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • No data is collected from viewers of the channel.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file also removes the app's access on that computer.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and the YouTube Terms of Service.
